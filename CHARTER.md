# Charter

## Purpose

Develop an open, portable, vendor-neutral, testable standard that helps ministries, institutions, projects, and individuals assess and safely engage AI agents created by others.

## Core principles

- Human- and machine-readable declarations
- Claims separated from evidence
- Capabilities separated from deployment permissions
- Explicit AI identity and accountable sponsor/operator
- Least privilege and risk-specific profiles
- Privacy, safeguarding, provenance, audit, incident response, and revocation
- Framework, model, vendor, marketplace, and chain neutrality
- Open governance and interoperable implementations
- No pay-to-trust or permanent universal reputation score

## Independence

The Empty Jars collaboration platform may implement this standard, but cannot privately redefine it. Other adopters must be able to implement it independently.

## Naming and trademarks

Protected Church terms are not used in the project or repository name. Descriptive references require restrained factual wording, non-affiliation language, attribution, and General Conference legal/trademark clearance before public release.
