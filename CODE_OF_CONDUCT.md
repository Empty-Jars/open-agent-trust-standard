# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, patient, transparent, and welcoming contributor community. Participation must support the mission of Empty Jars without coercion, harassment, discrimination, impersonation, manipulation, or abuse of authority.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with dignity and assume good faith while examining claims carefully.
- Disclose relevant conflicts, automation, and AI assistance.
- Keep criticism specific to ideas, evidence, behavior, or artifacts.
- Protect confidential, pastoral, personal, security, and institutional information.
- Follow project-scoped authority and do not claim organizational endorsement.
- Accept correction and support fair appeals.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, secret disclosure, fraud, impersonation, undisclosed conflicts, spam, reward manipulation, unauthorized access, or attempts to bypass review and authority controls are prohibited.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive reports in a public issue. Until a dedicated confidential channel is approved, contact an authorized repository steward directly. Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary, with an auditable explanation and appeal route where safe.
